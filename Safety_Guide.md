# ⚠️ Safety Guide

English | [中文](./安全指南.md)

---

**Warning**: Please read this guide carefully before operating OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid and use it safely. Use at your own risk.

Before using, installing, or debugging OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid, please ensure:

- You have completely read this brief version and the complete "Safety Statement" related chapters;
- You fully understand the possible mechanical, electrical, and software risks;
- You have prepared effective emergency stop devices and necessary personal protective measures.

---

## 🛡️ Safe Use Requirements

### 1. 📍 Install in a Safe Location

**Installation Location**

When installing OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid, please place it on a flat and stable ground, ensuring all 4 wheel hubs can touch the ground.

**Installation Points:**

- ✅ Choose a flat and stable surface
- ❌ Stay away from fragile items and flammable materials
- ❌ Stay away from moisture sources and water sources
- ❌ Avoid areas with frequent personnel traffic

---

### 2. 📏 Maintain Safe Distance (maintain at least 1.5 meters distance)

**Safe Distance**

When operating OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid, be sure to ensure that no part of your body or any object enters its range of motion.

**Distance Requirements:**

- 🚫 Stay outside the robot's range of motion during operation
- 🔌 Be sure to confirm that the power is turned off before adjustment
- 👥 Ensure nearby personnel will not accidentally turn on the power
- 🚧 Use obstacles or markers to restrict access when necessary

> **Warning**: The range of motion changes when handling large objects or installing the original end effector.

> **Warning**: When it is inevitable to approach OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid during teleoperation, maintain a safe distance from the elbow, shoulder, and end effector protrusions to avoid fingers getting caught.

---

### 3. 🥽 Wear Appropriate Protective Equipment (PPE)

**Protective Equipment**

Be sure to wear safety goggles when operating OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid.

**Essential Protective Equipment:**

- 👓 Safety goggles (must be worn)
- 👷 Safety helmet (recommended)
- 🧤 Protective gloves (wear when necessary)
- 👞 Safety shoes (recommended)
- 👔 Well-fitting work clothes (avoid loose clothing)

When you need to remotely operate the robot or for other purposes, wear well-fitting clothes to avoid being caught.

---

### 4. ⚖️ Operate Within Specified Limits

**Operating Limits**

Do not operate OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid beyond the specified payload limits for single arm or dual arms.

**Load Limits:**

- 📦 Single arm maximum load in MIT mode: 5kg; single arm maximum load in CSP mode: 2kg.
- 🤝 Dual arm collaboration maximum load in MIT: 8kg; dual arm collaboration maximum load: 5kg.
- 🔧 The weight of the end effector needs to be included when using it
- ⚠️ Overloading may cause equipment damage or safety accidents

---

### 5. 🛑 Prepare Emergency Stop

**Emergency Stop**

![Emergency Stop Button](./image/emergency-stop.png)

Familiarize yourself with the location and operation of the emergency stop device so that it can be activated immediately in case of abnormality.

**Emergency Stop Requirements:**

- 📍 A mechanical self-locking hardware emergency stop button is equipped; please place it in an obvious and easily accessible location
- 🏃 Participating personnel should be at a safe position far enough from OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid
- 🔄 Regularly test whether the emergency stop function is normal

> **Warning**:
>
> OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid has high rear-drive performance and is suitable for bilateral control.
> Please note that if the power is cut off due to an emergency stop, the held load will drop rapidly. Please ensure no one is near OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid

## 🔧 Safe Maintenance and Inspection

To ensure OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid operates safely, daily inspection and maintenance are crucial. Pay special attention to the following points to detect any abnormalities or wear early.

### 🔩 Base Fasteners and Loose Screws

**Risk Description:**

Repeated movements in certain postures or vibrations may cause screws or fixtures to loosen unexpectedly. Loose screws around the base or arm joints may lead to serious accidents.

**Inspection Points:**

- ✓ Check base fixing screws before each operation
- ✓ Check screws around arm joints
- ✓ Tighten immediately if looseness is found
- ✓ Regularly check tightening force with a torque wrench

---

### ⚙️ Damage to Mechanical Limits

**Risk Description:**

Each joint is equipped with mechanical limits to prevent abnormal postures and protect connecting wires. These mechanical limits may be deformed or destroyed by strong impacts.

**Inspection Points:**

- ✓ Regularly check mechanical limit devices
- ✓ Observe for deformation or cracks
- ✓ Test if the limit function is normal
- ✓ Replace immediately if damage is found

---

### 🔊 Abnormal Sounds or Joint Jamming

**Risk Description:**

Overloading or impacts during operation may cause abnormal noise or joint jamming. This is usually caused by deformation of machined or sheet metal parts, motor gearbox damage, or cables getting caught.

**Inspection Points:**

- ✓ Listen for abnormal sounds during operation
- ✓ Check if joint movement is smooth
- ✓ Determine the specific location of noise or jamming
- ✓ Thoroughly investigate the cause and handle it promptly

---

### 🔌 Wire and Connector Damage

**Risk Description:**

Repeated sharp bends or improper disconnections can damage wires and connectors. If cables or connectors are damaged, OpenFleX-Wheeled-Humanoid may not work properly and may cause electrical problems such as damage to power or control equipment.

**Inspection Points:**

- ✓ Check cables for wear or damage
- ✓ Check connectors for looseness or deformation
- ✓ Check the complete wiring from base to end
- ✓ Cut power immediately and replace damaged parts if abnormalities are found
